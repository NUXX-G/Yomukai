Youngest Son of the Renowned Swordsmanship Clan

Swordmaster's Youngest Son | The Youngest Son of a Renowned Family of Swordsmen | 검술명가 막내아들

Ongoing

[From the studio of The Archmage Returns After 4000 Years] Jin Runkandel The youngest son of Runkandel, the Continent's best Swordsmanship Clan And the worst dunce in all of Runkandel's history. As he is miserably driven out of his clan and meets his pitiful death, another chance is given to him. 'How do you want to use this power?' 'I want to use the power for myself.' With his memory of his past life and his overwhelming talent, and a contract with a god The preparation to become the strongest is completed.
